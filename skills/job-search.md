# Job Search

> AVOID HALLUCINATIONS, GUESSWORK, PARAPHRASING
> USE DIRECTLY QUOTED INFORMATION IN ALL TASKS

Using the following tools:

- Job Preferences (through supabase mcp - personal info)
- The Job board list in this document
- Application history (through supabase mcp)
- Email history (through gmail through composio mcp)

1. Look for the top 25 job postings given the job preferences. Always prioritize recent listings.

2. For each selected job, research the company, and create a 1 page md in `temp/job_posts`. At the top use a grid with short details:
   - job link
   - job portal
   - job title
   - salary
   - company
   - company size
   - industry

3. In the next section give an abbreviated version of the job desscription.
   - Use direct quotes only and focus on responsibilities and requirements (make exceptions for relevant details. make exceptions for commonality with job preferences.).
   - Include the project or team age if stated.

4. In the last section provide a summary of the company in short language.
   - Include any information you were able to find about them online.
   - Include key factors like:
     - company age
     - company size
     - where the company is registered
     - company mission
     - company outlook
