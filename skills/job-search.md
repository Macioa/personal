# Job Search

> AVOID HALLUCINATIONS, GUESSWORK, PARAPHRASING
> USE DIRECTLY QUOTED INFORMATION IN ALL TASKS
> REVIEW ATOMIC.MD FOR OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS

Inputs:
- Job preferences
- Job board list in this document
- Application history
- Email history

1. EasyPlaywrightMCP — find the top 25 job postings for the job preferences. Always prioritize recent listings. Search multiple sites. Use Supabase application history to avoid sites scoured too heavily recently. Do select job posts that have already been applied to. Ideal posts: 0–3 days old.

2. For each selected job, research the company and create a 1-page md in `temp/job_posts`. Top: grid with short details:
   - job link
   - job portal
   - job title
   - salary
   - company
   - company size
   - industry

3. Next section: abbreviated job description.
   - Direct quotes only; focus on responsibilities and requirements (exceptions for relevant details and commonality with job preferences).
   - Include project or team age if stated.

4. Last section: short company summary.
   - Include any information found online.
   - Key factors:
     - company age
     - company size
     - where the company is registered
     - company mission
     - company outlook
